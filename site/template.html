Mapping the semiconductor supply chain from public filings

A directed supplier-to-customer graph of listed semiconductor companies, with the source and filing date of every edge, and tests of how shocks and returns move across tiers. Negative and uninformative results are reported as such.

Method notes and limitations

Prices are daily adjusted closes aligned to US trading days by exchange close time, converted to US dollars. Tier indices use current market capitalisation as static weights, so the sample is subject to survivorship. The edge set is small; statistics that depend on it (customer momentum, linked-company event studies) are not yet reported as results. No price series are published on this site.
